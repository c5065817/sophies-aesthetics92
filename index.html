About Sophie

Clinical experience with a personal approach.

Sophie is a Registered Nurse and GP Practice Nurse who provides aesthetic treatments alongside her clinical nursing role.

Her approach places consultation, patient safety and realistic expectations at the centre of aesthetic care. Each client is assessed individually before treatment is considered.

The aim is not to create a standardised appearance, but to provide considered treatment that respects each person's individual features and goals.

Services

Care centred around the individual.

Every treatment pathway begins with a consultation to discuss your goals, relevant medical history and treatment suitability.

01 — CONSULTATION

Aesthetic Consultation

A one-to-one discussion to understand your concerns and determine whether treatment is suitable for you.

Your consultation provides an opportunity to discuss your goals, medical history and expectations before making any treatment decision.

There is no obligation to proceed with a treatment following a consultation.

02 — TREATMENT

Aesthetic Treatments

Individually planned aesthetic care following appropriate clinical assessment and discussion.

Treatment options are discussed according to individual goals, clinical suitability and realistic expectations.

Treatment recommendations and pricing are confirmed following consultation.

03 — AFTERCARE

Follow-up & Aftercare

Appropriate advice and support continue after treatment rather than ending when you leave the clinic.

Clear aftercare information is provided following treatment, including relevant guidance and when to seek further advice.

Follow-up can be arranged when clinically appropriate.

The approach

Professional care beyond the treatment itself.

Aesthetic treatment should begin with careful assessment rather than simply selecting a procedure.

01. Consultation

Discuss your goals, concerns, medical history and expectations.

02. Assessment

Consider suitability, potential benefits, limitations and relevant risks.

03. Treatment

Proceed with an individually agreed plan where treatment is appropriate.

04. Aftercare

Receive clear aftercare advice and appropriate follow-up support.
