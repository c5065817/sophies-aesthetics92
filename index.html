About Sophie

Clinical experience with a personal approach.

Sophie is a Registered Nurse and GP Practice Nurse who provides aesthetic services alongside her clinical nursing role.

Her approach places consultation, safety and realistic expectations at the centre of the client experience. Each person is considered individually before any treatment decision is made.

The aim is not to create a standardised appearance, but to provide considered care that respects the individual's features, preferences and goals.

Services

Care centred around the individual.

Every pathway begins with a conversation about expectations, suitability and the options available.

01 — Consultation

Aesthetic consultation

A structured discussion designed to understand your goals, relevant history and expectations.

Consultation provides an opportunity to ask questions, discuss suitability and consider options before deciding whether to proceed.

There is no obligation to continue with a treatment following the consultation.

02 — Treatment

Aesthetic treatments

Individually planned aesthetic care following appropriate consultation and assessment.

Treatment options are considered according to individual goals, clinical suitability and realistic expectations.

Specific recommendations would be discussed during consultation.

03 — Aftercare

Follow-up & aftercare

Clear guidance and appropriate support following treatment.

Appropriate aftercare information supports recovery and helps clients understand when further advice may be required.

Follow-up can be arranged where appropriate.

The approach

More than the treatment itself.

The process is designed around assessment, communication and appropriate follow-up.
