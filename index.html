About Sophie

Sophie is a 34-year-old Registered Nurse and experienced healthcare professional. She currently works as a GP Practice Nurse alongside providing aesthetic treatments on a part-time basis.

Her clinical background has developed strong skills in patient assessment, communication and safe clinical practice. She brings this healthcare experience into aesthetics, with an emphasis on professional standards, appropriate treatment and achieving natural-looking results.

Approach to Aesthetics

Sophie's approach is centred on individual assessment and providing treatments that are appropriate to each client. Patient safety, informed decision-making and realistic outcomes remain central to her practice.
